# WIKI-102 — Brand teal for the portal search button

**Type:** Visual change · **Priority:** Medium · **Reporter:** Design

## User story

As part of the 2026 brand refresh, the **portal** (www.wikipedia.org) should use the new brand
color for its primary action, so the entry page matches the new brand.

## Scope

- **Portal page only** — the "Search" button next to the search box.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The portal's "Search" button background and border are `color-brand-primary` (`#0E7C7B`).
2. Its magnifier icon stays white (`color-inverted`).
3. Applies on **desktop and phone**.

## Must not change

- **Article pages, including their header "Search" button.** Article pages keep their current
  look; they are covered by a later ticket (WIKI-110).
- The search input, its size and position, and everything else on the portal.
