# WIKI-103 — Hide the App Store badge on phones

**Type:** Visual change · **Priority:** Low · **Reporter:** Mobile team

## User story

On phones we already detect the device and link straight to the right store, so the
"Download on the App Store" badge in the portal's "Download Wikipedia" box is redundant there.

## Scope

- **Portal page, phone breakpoint (≤ 480px) only.**

## Acceptance criteria

1. On phone, the "Download on the App Store" badge is hidden; the Google Play badge stays and is centered.

## Must not change

- **Desktop and tablet: both badges stay visible**, exactly as today.
- Everything else on the portal.
