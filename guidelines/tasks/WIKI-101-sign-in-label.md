# WIKI-101 — Rename "Log in" to "Sign in"

**Type:** Content change · **Priority:** Low · **Reporter:** Product

## User story

As a reader, I want the account link to say "Sign in", matching the wording used across our apps.

## Scope

- **Article pages**, header link "Log in" → "Sign in".

## Acceptance criteria

1. The header link reads **"Sign in"** (sentence case) on desktop and phone.
2. Neighbouring header links ("Donate", "Create account") may shift slightly to make room — nothing else.

## Must not change

- The portal page.
- Any other text, color or layout on article pages.
