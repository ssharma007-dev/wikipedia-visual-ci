# WIKI-1 — Brand teal for the portal search button

**Type:** Task · **Priority:** - · **Status:** In Progress · **Reporter:** Shreya Sharma
**Source:** Jira — https://testing-visual-ci.atlassian.net/browse/WIKI-1
**Labels:** brand-refresh, visual

### User story

As part of the 2026 brand refresh, the portal (www.wikipedia.org) should use the new brand color for its primary action, so the entry page matches the new brand.

### Scope

- Portal page only — the "Search" button next to the search box.

### Acceptance criteria

1. The portal "Search" button background and border use the brand color #0E7C7B (color-brand-primary).
2. Its magnifier icon stays white.
3. Applies on desktop and phone.

### Must not change

- Article pages, including their header "Search" button — they keep their current look (covered by a later ticket).
- The search input, its size and position, and everything else on the portal.
