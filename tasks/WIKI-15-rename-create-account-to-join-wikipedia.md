### User story

As a new reader, I want a friendlier invitation to sign up, so more readers join the community.

### Scope

- Article pages, header link "Create account".

### Acceptance criteria

1. The header link reads "Join Wikipedia" (sentence case) on desktop and phone.
2. Neighbouring header links may shift slightly to make room.

### Must not change

- The link's color and style.
- Every other header link ("Donate", "Log in").
- The portal page.
