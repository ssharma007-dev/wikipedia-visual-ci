#### Why

We are moving iOS users to the mobile website, so the portal should no longer promote the iOS app.

#### Should change

- On the Wikipedia portal page (www.wikipedia.org), in the "Download Wikipedia" box at the bottom, the "Download on the App Store" badge is removed.

#### Must not change

- The "Get it on Google Play" badge stays, in the same place.
- The text "Download Wikipedia for Android or iOS" stays as it is (a separate ticket updates it).
- The Wikipedia logo, the tagline, the search bar and the Search button, on every device.
- The language links around the globe.
- The article pages and the "not found" page.
